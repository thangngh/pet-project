## Architecture conformance

- Domain layer pure: no package imports under any `domain/`. Verified.
- No controller imports a concrete service class.
- `RequestIdentity` has one definition, in
  `shared/adapters/request-context/request-context.types.ts`.
- Cross-context delivery goes through the outbox, never `EventBus`.
- Contexts `auth` and `catalog` have outbox tables; `user` and `product`
  publish nothing and deliberately have none.

**Known drift, documented not fixed:** Auth has no `use-cases/` (its features
are methods on `AuthService`); no context has a handling centre or a CQRS
split; `ConflictError` and the `ApplicationError` / `InfrastructureError`
families do not exist. All four are marked `[plan]` in `CLAUDE.md`.
